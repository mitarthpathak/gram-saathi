# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

## Overview

PMUY gives LPG cooking gas connections to women from poor households, so they can cook without
smoke from wood, cow-dung cakes or kerosene.

## Benefits

A deposit-free LPG connection in the woman's name. Under Ujjwala 2.0 the first refill and the gas
stove are also given free. Ujjwala beneficiaries get a subsidy on refills, credited to their bank
account.

## Eligibility

An adult woman (18 years or older) from a poor household, where nobody in the household already
has an LPG connection. Poor households include SC/ST families, PMAY-Gramin and Antyodaya (AAY)
beneficiaries, forest dwellers, Most Backward Classes, tea and ex-tea garden tribes, people
living on islands, and SECC 2011 households. Other poor families can apply with a written
declaration.

## How to apply

Apply at the nearest LPG distributor or online at pmuy.gov.in. The connection is released after
KYC.

## Documents needed

Aadhaar of the applicant, ration card or another document showing the family, bank account
details and address proof. Migrant families can give a self-declaration of address.
