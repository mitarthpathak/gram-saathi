# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

## Eligibility

An eligible beneficiary is an adult woman from a poor household who submits the scheme's
deprivation declaration. The household must not already have an LPG connection registered to
any family member listed in its family-composition document. A PMUY connection is issued in the
name of an adult woman.

## Benefits

The official FAQ describes a security-deposit-free LPG connection with no installation charge,
a free first refill, and a free two-burner stove. The FAQ says the beneficiary does not pay the
distributor for these listed items.

## How to apply

Apply online using the form on pmuy.gov.in, submit an application at an LPG distributorship, or
ask a nearby Common Service Centre for help. The FAQ says online self-submission has no fee; a
₹20 charge applies when a Common Service Centre submits the application.

## Documents needed

The FAQ lists the applicant's application and KYC form, recent photograph, and Aadhaar; Aadhaar
details for adult family members shown on the family-composition document; address proof; bank
account details; a ration card or other government family-composition document; and the signed
deprivation declaration. Migrant applicants may use the prescribed self-declaration for address
and family composition.

## Verification

Biometric Aadhaar authentication is mandatory and is carried out by the LPG distributor.
