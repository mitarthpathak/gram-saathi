# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G)

## Overview

PMAY-G helps poor rural families who have no house, or live in a kutcha or broken-down house, to
build a pucca house.

## Benefits

Financial help of ₹1.20 lakh in plain areas and ₹1.30 lakh in hilly states, difficult areas and
IAP districts, paid in instalments directly into the beneficiary's bank account. Beneficiaries
can also get help to build a toilet under Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin) and up to 90–95 days of
paid MGNREGA work for building their own house.

## Eligibility

Families are selected from the SECC 2011 data and the Awaas+ survey, and the list is checked by
the Gram Sabha. Houseless families and families living in one- or two-room kutcha houses get
priority.

## How to apply

There is no separate application form. Check whether your name is on the PMAY-G list at your Gram
Panchayat or on pmayg.nic.in. If your family is eligible but not on the list, ask the Gram
Panchayat about the Awaas+ survey.

## Documents needed

Aadhaar, bank account details, MGNREGA job card and Swachh Bharat Mission number, if available.
