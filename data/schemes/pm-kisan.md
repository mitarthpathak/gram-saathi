# PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

## Overview

PM-KISAN is a Central Sector scheme funded by the Government of India. State and Union Territory
administrations identify the farmer families eligible under the scheme guidelines. For this
scheme, a family means husband, wife and minor children.

## Benefits

Eligible landholding farmer families receive ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments. The
money is transferred directly to beneficiaries' bank accounts. eKYC is mandatory for registered
farmers.

## Eligibility

State and Union Territory administrations identify eligible farmer families under the scheme
guidelines and land records. Institutional landholders are excluded.

The portal lists these exclusion categories: current or former holders of constitutional posts;
current or former ministers, legislators, municipal corporation mayors and district panchayat
chairs; serving or retired government, public sector and regular local body employees, except
Multi Tasking Staff and Class IV / Group D employees; retired pensioners receiving at least
₹10,000 per month, with the same employee exception; people who paid income tax in the last
assessment year; and registered professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered
accountants and architects who practise their profession.

## How to apply

The PM-KISAN portal provides New Farmer Registration and a Know Your Status service. The portal
also says State and Union Territory administrations identify eligible families. eKYC is
mandatory for registered farmers to receive scheme instalments.

## Status checks

The portal says benefits may be temporarily withheld for suspected cases such as land ownership
acquired after 1 February 2019 or more than one family member receiving benefits, until physical
verification is completed. It directs farmers to check Know Your Status on the PM-KISAN website
or mobile app for details.
