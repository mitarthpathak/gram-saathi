# PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

## Overview

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a central government scheme that gives direct
income support to landholding farmer families.

## Benefits

Eligible landholding farmer families get ₹6,000 per year, paid in three equal instalments of
₹2,000 directly into their Aadhaar-seeded bank account (DBT).

## Eligibility

All landholding farmer families whose names are in the land records are eligible. Excluded:
institutional landholders, families with an income-tax payer, serving or retired government
employees (except Group D / MTS), registered professionals such as doctors, engineers and
lawyers, and pensioners getting ₹10,000 or more per month.

## How to apply

Registration is free. Apply at pmkisan.gov.in (New Farmer Registration), at a Common Service
Centre (CSC) or through the local Patwari / revenue officer. eKYC with Aadhaar is mandatory to
receive instalments.

## Documents needed

Aadhaar card, land ownership records and a bank account linked to Aadhaar.

## Helpline

PM-KISAN helpline: 155261. Payment status can be checked on pmkisan.gov.in under "Know Your
Status".
