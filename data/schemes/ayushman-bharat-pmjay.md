# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: Senior Citizens Aged 70 or Above

## Scope

This note covers the PM-JAY senior-citizen benefit described in the National Health Authority's
FAQ. It does not describe other PM-JAY eligibility groups.

## Eligibility

All senior citizens aged 70 or above are eligible for this benefit regardless of economic
status. Age is determined from the date of birth recorded in Aadhaar.

## Benefits

Eligible senior citizens can receive free medical treatment up to ₹5 lakh under the scheme. The
National Health Authority FAQ says treatment can be accessed from the first day of enrolment,
without a waiting period.

## How to apply

Aadhaar-based eKYC is required for enrolment and issuance of an Ayushman card. The FAQ says
Aadhaar is the only document needed for this enrolment. Apply through beneficiary.nha.gov.in or
the Ayushman App. Eligible family members aged 70 or above can be added after the first member
is enrolled.
