# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY

## Overview

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) is a government health assurance
scheme run by the National Health Authority.

## Benefits

PM-JAY gives health cover of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary
hospital care. Treatment is cashless and paperless at empanelled public and private hospitals.

## Eligibility

Rural families are covered under the SECC 2011 deprivation criteria and urban families under
occupational criteria. All senior citizens aged 70 and above are covered irrespective of income,
as are ASHA and Anganwadi workers and their families.

## How to apply

Check eligibility at beneficiary.nha.gov.in or call 14555. The Ayushman card is made free of
cost with Aadhaar eKYC at a CSC, at an empanelled hospital (Ayushman Mitra desk) or on the
Ayushman app.

## Documents needed

Aadhaar card, and a ration card or other proof that the family is on the beneficiary list.

## Helpline

National Health Authority helpline: 14555.
