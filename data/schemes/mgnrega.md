# Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGS)

## Employment guarantee

The official scheme website says MGNREGS provides a legal guarantee of at least 100 days of
wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to
do unskilled manual work.

## Scope

This note covers only the employment guarantee stated on the official scheme website. It does
not describe registration steps, wage rates, unemployment allowance or local implementation
rules.
