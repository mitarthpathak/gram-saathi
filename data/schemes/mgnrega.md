# Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme (MGNREGA)

## Overview

MGNREGA is a law that guarantees paid work to rural households whose adult members are willing
to do unskilled manual work.

## Benefits

At least 100 days of wage work per household in a financial year. Work is given within 5 km of
the village where possible; if it is farther, 10% extra wages are paid. Wages are paid directly
into a bank or post office account, normally within 15 days. If work is not given within 15 days
of asking for it, the worker is entitled to an unemployment allowance.

## Eligibility

Any rural household whose adult members (18 years or older) are willing to do unskilled manual
work. Each household needs a job card.

## How to apply

Apply for a job card at the Gram Panchayat, in writing or orally. The job card is free and
should be issued within 15 days. Then ask the Gram Panchayat for work in writing and keep the
dated receipt.

## Wages

Wage rates are fixed separately for each state and revised every year. Men and women get the
same wage.

## Complaints

Complaints can be made at the Gram Panchayat, to the Programme Officer at the block office, or on
the MGNREGA website nrega.nic.in.
